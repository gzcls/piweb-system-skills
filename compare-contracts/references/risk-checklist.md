# Risk Review Checklist

Apply this checklist from the declared review perspective. A clause is not inherently favorable or unfavorable without knowing which party bears the obligation or receives the right.

## Commercial and Measurement

- Are all fees, currencies, taxes, expense rules, and adjustment mechanisms stated?
- Do minimum commitments, volume bands, or dependencies make the headline price misleading?
- Are service-credit calculations, fee caps, and exclusivity effects comparable on the same basis?
- Can one party change price, scope, assumptions, or policy unilaterally?

## Cash Flow

- What triggers invoicing and when is payment due?
- Are disputed invoices, set-off, withholding, deposits, prepayment, interest, and suspension rights addressed?
- Do acceptance delays or missing evidence postpone payment indefinitely?

## Performance and Control

- Are deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, KPIs, and remedies measurable?
- Are dependencies and relief events defined, with notification and mitigation duties?
- Can work be subcontracted or assigned, and who remains accountable?
- Are change control, governance, reporting, audit, and record-retention processes workable?

## Duration and Exit

- Is renewal automatic, and is the notice window operationally realistic?
- Which party may terminate for convenience, cause, insolvency, prolonged force majeure, or repeated failure?
- Are cure periods asymmetric or impractical?
- Are transition assistance, data return, asset return, final charges, refunds, and survival obligations stated?

## Liability and Indemnity

- Is the liability cap mutual, aggregate, per claim, annual, or tied to a fee period?
- Which claims are outside the cap, and are exclusions internally consistent?
- Are indirect-loss exclusions undermined by broad indemnities or carve-outs?
- Are defense control, notice, settlement consent, mitigation, and third-party claim procedures defined?
- Does required insurance match the allocated risks?

## Data, Confidentiality, IP, and Compliance

- Are controller/processor roles, permitted uses, security standards, breach notice, transfers, deletion, and audits stated?
- Does confidentiality cover duration, permitted recipients, compelled disclosure, return, and residual knowledge?
- Who owns background IP, deliverables, modifications, data, and feedback?
- Are licenses sufficient for intended operations and transition?
- Are anti-bribery, sanctions, export, sector rules, accessibility, employment, or modern-slavery duties relevant?

## Legal Mechanics

- Is the contract hierarchy clear when the main agreement, order form, SLA, and policies conflict?
- Are warranties, disclaimers, force majeure, notices, assignment, waiver, severability, governing law, venue, and dispute escalation coherent?
- Do incorporated online policies change without consent or archival evidence?
- Are signature authority, execution date, amendments, and handwritten changes reliable?

## Severity Guidance

- Critical: plausible threat to legality, continuity, control of core data/IP, or uncapped material exposure.
- High: material financial or operational exposure likely to affect the decision or negotiation.
- Medium: meaningful ambiguity, imbalance, or process weakness with manageable mitigations.
- Low: drafting clarity, housekeeping, or limited-impact inconsistency.

Severity must include a rationale and confidence. Do not turn this guidance into a numeric score without an approved weighting model.
